Campfire games

For travel, road trips and campfires. No internet, no problem: one phone shares a hotspot, everyone joins it, and the host's phone runs the game for the whole group.

1

Host turns on a hotspot and taps "Host game". On Android the app can create a local-only hotspot itself; on iPhone the host switches on Personal Hotspot in Settings (the app shows how).

2

Players scan the QR code. It holds the Wi-Fi name, password and room code, so one scan joins the network and the room. Manual code entry is the fallback.

3

The host phone runs a small local server (WebSocket over the hotspot). It deals roles and words, runs timers, counts votes. No data leaves the group.

4

Any shared Wi-Fi also works — a car's Wi-Fi, a guesthouse router. iOS asks once for "Local Network" permission.

Imposter

Everyone gets the same secret word except the imposter, who only sees the category. Take turns saying one clue, then vote.

3–15 players · 5–10 min · word packs in UZ / RU / EN

Mafia

Host picks roles, each phone gets one secret role. The app narrates nights and days, wakes roles and counts votes.

6–15 players · 20–40 min · no narrator needed

Who am I?

Every player gets a secret character. Your phone shows everyone's character except yours. Ask yes/no questions to guess it.

3–12 players · 10–20 min

Would you rather

Two options, everyone votes on their own phone at once, then the split and who picked what is revealed. Great for icebreakers.

2–15 players · endless · spicy pack off by default

Secrets stay secret

1

The host sends each secret only to that player's phone — never broadcast to the room.

2

Hold to reveal: secrets show only while a thumb is on the card, then hide again.

3

Mafia nights: every phone gets a task at the same time, so nobody can tell roles from who is tapping.

4

The host plays too: the host phone keeps the host's own secret hidden like everyone else's.

Resilience

1

Reconnect: a player who drops off rejoins with the same seat and secret.

2

Host battery low: warn at 20%. The game state is saved on the host, so a restart resumes.

3

One phone only? Pass-and-play mode for Imposter, Who am I and Would you rather.

4

Night-friendly: dark UI, dim brightness, big text you can read by firelight.
