Feed & Top Shots

Explorers post photos from their trips — solo, with a group or on a tour — up to 10 per post. Every two weeks the 10 most-liked posts become Top Shots: their authors get XP, a badge and a spot on the winners' wall.

Post a trip

1–10 photos, caption, place tag; solo, group or tour.

Collect likes

Likes from real accounts count during the 2-week round.

Top 10 crowned

Every second Sunday, 23:59 Tashkent.

Rewards

XP, Top Shot badge, winners' wall, profile frame.

Inspire a trip

Every post links to its place — "Start adventure here".

Who can win

1

Posted during the round (Mon 00:00 to Sunday-after-next 23:59, Tashkent time).

2

Tagged with a place, and the author has a verified check-in there — so winning photos are from real trips.

3

Own photos only. Reposts and tour operators' marketing posts are excluded (operators get their own "Tour stories" tab).

4

Ties: the post that reached the count first wins.

Fair likes

!

Only likes from accounts older than 7 days with a verified phone count toward Top Shots.

!

No self-likes. Like rings and sudden spikes from new accounts are flagged and reviewed before winners are announced.

!

The leaderboard shows rank, not exact counts, in the last 24 hours to cool down last-minute campaigns.

Safety & places

✓

Photo GPS data is stripped. Fragile spots show only the region name, never a pin.

✓

Posts showing risky behaviour (no helmet on scrambles, swimming in "Not now" conditions) can be reported and are hidden from Top Shots.

✓

Tagged people must approve before their name appears.
